1. Hang out (待在一起/闲逛/聚聚)
Do you want to hang out at the mall this weekend?

We usually just hang out at my house after school.

I spent the whole afternoon hanging out with my friends.

They like to hang out at the park when the weather is nice.

I don't have plans; I'm just going to hang out at home.

2. Up to (正在做/忙于)
Hey, what have you been up to lately?

What are you up to this weekend?

I’m not up to much, just watching some TV.

He looks suspicious; I think he's up to something.

It’s up to you to decide where we eat dinner.

3. Take it easy (放松/别紧张/休息)
You’ve been working so hard; you should take it easy this weekend.

Take it easy! It was just a small mistake.

I’m just going to stay home and take it easy tonight.

The doctor told him to take it easy for a few days after the surgery.

Bye! Take it easy and have a great trip!

4. Give someone a hand (帮个忙)
Could you give me a hand with these heavy boxes?

I'm happy to give you a hand with your homework if you're stuck.

Can someone give me a hand moving this table?

He gave her a hand fixing her flat tire.

I need someone to give me a hand in the kitchen.

5. Check out (去看看/看看这个)
You should check out that new cafe down the street.

Check out this cool video I found online!

I need to check out the price of the tickets before I book.

Let’s go check out the sale at the department store.

Have you checked out the new movie everyone is talking about?

6. Make sense (有道理/说得通)
What he said doesn't make sense to me at all.

Does that make sense, or should I explain it again?

It makes sense to save money while you're still young.

The story is a bit confusing; it doesn't quite make sense.

It finally makes sense why she was so upset yesterday.

7. Run out of (用完了/用尽了)
We ran out of milk, so I need to go to the store.

I’m afraid I’ve run out of time to finish the presentation.

My phone is about to run out of battery.

We’re running out of ideas for the new project.

I hope we don't run out of gas before we reach the station.

8. Find out (发现/弄清楚/查明)
I need to find out what time the meeting starts.

How did you find out about the surprise party?

We’ll find out the exam results next Monday.

I’m trying to find out why the internet isn't working.

She was shocked to find out the truth about the situation.

9. Get along (与某人相处融洽)
Do you get along with your new coworkers?

My brother and I didn't get along when we were kids.

It’s important to get along with your neighbors.

They seem to get along really well together.

I hope everyone in the team can get along and work together.

10. Look forward to (期待)
I look forward to meeting you in person.

We are looking forward to our vacation next month.

I look forward to hearing from you soon.

The kids are looking forward to the school trip.

I'm really looking forward to the weekend.

11. Kind of / Sort of (有点儿/大概)
I’m kind of tired, so I might go to bed early.

It was sort of expensive, but I bought it anyway.

I kind of like the new song, but it's not my favorite.

The movie was sort of boring at the beginning.

I’m kind of hungry; do you want to grab a snack?

12. No big deal (没什么大不了/没事儿)
Don't worry about being late; it's no big deal.

I lost my pen, but it's no big deal; I have another one.

It’s no big deal if you can't come to the party tonight.

He made a mistake, but it was really no big deal.

Thanks for the help! — Oh, it's no big deal.

13. Figure out (想明白/解决)
I can't figure out how to use this new app.

We need to figure out a way to save more money.

It took me a while to figure out the answer to the puzzle.

Can you help me figure out what’s wrong with my car?

I’m still trying to figure out my plans for next year.

14. Keep in touch (保持联系)
We should definitely keep in touch after the course ends.

Let's keep in touch via email or social media.

I try to keep in touch with my old friends from college.

It’s hard to keep in touch when everyone is so busy.

Goodbye! Make sure to keep in touch!
